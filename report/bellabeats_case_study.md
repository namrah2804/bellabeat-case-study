# Bellabeat Marketing Analytics Case Study

## 1. Business Task
Bellabeat aims to expand its position in the global smart wellness market by leveraging data-driven marketing insights. The purpose of this analysis is to explore smart device usage data to better understand consumer behaviour patterns related to activity, sleep, and overall wellness. These findings will be applied to a selected Bellabeat product in order to provide actionable, high-level marketing strategy recommendations.

## 2. Stakeholders
The primary stakeholders for this analysis include:
- Urška Sršen (Chief Creative Officer), who is interested in using data insights to guide brand growth and product marketing.
- Sando Mur (Executive Team Member), who focuses on strategic decision-making and business expansion.
- The Bellabeat marketing analytics team, who will use the insights to support campaign planning and performance tracking.
